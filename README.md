5. DUAL FUEL + RECD TECHNOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------

Create a technical but easy-to-understand section.

Title:

“Dual Fuel & RECD Technology”

Explain the two technologies based on the PDF.

Dual Fuel:
Controlled injection of gaseous/liquid fuel into the diesel engine's airflow to replace a portion of diesel.

RECD:
A particulate trap/mechanism added to the diesel engine exhaust with controller, sensors and arrangements to periodically clean the filters.

Explain that dual-fuel kits help reduce particulate generation during combustion, while RECD traps particulates from diesel engine exhaust.

Use the relevant diagrams/images from the PDF.
